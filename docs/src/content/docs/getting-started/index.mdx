Harpy is a data processing software suite. The different commands are intended to seamlessly work
with each other with minimal friction. You may have a particular plan for your linked-read data,
and the workflow you need will vary depending on your project. There are generally two kinds of workflows
that you would consider for your work: one for variant detection and the other for genome assembly.

<Aside title="Harpy works with WGS">

While harpy is written primarily for linked-read data, most of the workflows accommodate non linked-read
data, such as qc, aligning, variant calling. You're encouraged to try harpy on your data, regardless of
whether it's linked reads or not.

</Aside>

## Typical Workflows
Depending on your project goals, you may want any combination of SNPs, structural
variants (inversions, deletions, duplications), or phased haplotypes. Below are diagrams
outlining general workflows for linked-read data, depending on your goals.

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Variant Calling">
<Steps>
1. **Preprocess data**
    
    Sample demultiplexing and linked-read barcode demultiplexing to identify barcodes remove technical sequences

2. **Quality control**

    Remove adapters, low quality sequences, reads that are too short, poly-G tails, etc.

3. **Align sequences**

    Align sequences to a reference genome, which enables variant detection later

4. **Call SNPs**

    Call Single Nucleotide Polymorphisms and small indels from alignments

5. **Impute genotypes** _optional_

    Use existing data to heuristically fill missing data or convert genotype likelihoods into hard genotype calls

6. **Phase variants into haplotypes**

    Convert individual SNPs into multi-allele haplotypes reflecting alleles that were inherited together from each parent

7. **Call structural variants**

    Call structural variants (SV) like inversions, large deletions, and duplications from alignments

</Steps>
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Assembly">
<Steps>
1. **Preprocess data**
    
    Sample demultiplexing and linked-read barcode demultiplexing to identify barcodes remove technical sequences

2. **Quality Control**

    Remove adapters, low quality sequences, reads that are too short, poly-G tails, etc.

3. **Deconvolute**
    Correct linked-read barcodes for unrelated sequences that share the same barcode by chance ("clashing")

4. Assembly

    Assemble sequences into a genome or metagenome
</Steps>
</TabItem>
</Tabs>
